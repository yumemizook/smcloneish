Help & About

About WebSM

WebSM is a browser-based emulation of Stepmania (or more exactly, Etterna, considering the tailored game mechanics)

Version: R1 preview

Why does this exist?

Well, I was just bored and decided to make a clone of Stepmania, but in the browser.
Yes, I know there are already multiple web-based Stepmania clones, some are even more well-made than this one. Unfortunately, such clones felt sluggish or outright uncomfortable for a keyboard player.

In case you don't know what Etterna is - it is an actively developed fork of Stepmania, optimized for keyboard play with lots of improvements. It introduced a lot of new features, most of which were nigh essential for keyboard players: MSD (difficulty calculation with skillset breakdown), Wife or MSS (millisecond-based scoring), and a corrected timing window table.
This is created as a solution to the Stepmania developers shitting on OG keyboard players and ignoring requests for optimazation.

Skillset Definitions

Stream
Continuous stream of notes (usually 1/16ths) without breaks.
Jumpstream
Streams containing jumps (2 notes at once), requiring coordination.
Handstream
Streams containing hands (3 notes at once), testing bracket ability.
Chordjack
Dense, repetitive chord patterns. Often tests reading and physical stamina.
Technical
Note snaps that are considered awkward for the difficulty calculator.
Stamina
The strain of the chart on the player over time.

Terminology

NPS (Notes Per Second)
Density of the chart. Peak NPS is the highest density reached.
SSR (Standardized Score Rating)
Numerical difficulty value calculated based on note density and patterns.
MA (Marvelous Attack)
Ratio of Marvelous judgments to Perfect judgments.
PA (Perfect Attack)
Ratio of Perfect judgments to Great judgments.
Simfile
The folder containing the song audio and stepchart (.sm/.ssc) file.
